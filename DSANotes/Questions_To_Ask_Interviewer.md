# 🎯 Reverse Interviewing: High-Signal Questions to Ask the Interviewer

> Inspired by **Yangshun Tay's [Tech Interview Handbook](https://github.com/yangshun/tech-interview-handbook)**.

At the end of every 45-minute technical interview, interviewers typically leave 5 minutes and ask:  
> *"Do you have any questions for me?"*

Saying *"No, I'm good"* or asking generic questions (*"What is your typical day like?"*) is a missed opportunity. High-caliber engineers use this time to **signal seniority, engineering maturity, team empathy, and architectural curiosity**.

Pick **2 to 3 targeted questions** from the categories below based on whether your interviewer is a Software Engineer, Tech Lead, or Engineering Manager.

---

## 🚀 1. Engineering Velocity, CI/CD & Deployments

These questions show you care about developer tooling, operational efficiency, and shipping without friction:

* *"What does the deployment pipeline look like from merging a pull request to hitting production? How often do you release, and how long does a build take?"*
* *"What kind of automated testing gates (unit, integration, canary deployments) are run before code hits 100% of traffic?"*
* *"If a critical bug is pushed to production, what is the rollback or roll-forward mechanism?"*

---

## 🛠️ 2. Technical Debt & Architectural Evolution

These questions demonstrate that you understand real-world software maintenance beyond LeetCode:

* *"How does the team balance shipping new feature requests against addressing architectural debt and refactoring?"*
* *"What is the single biggest technical bottleneck or legacy constraint that your team is actively trying to migrate away from?"*
* *"When choosing between a quick tactical fix and a long-term architectural redesign, how does your engineering team make that trade-off?"*

---

## 🚨 3. Incidents, On-Call & Reliability

These questions reveal the team's engineering health and post-incident culture:

* *"How is on-call structured for this team? What is the paging frequency during off-hours?"*
* *"How does the team conduct post-mortems after an outage? Is there a blameless culture focused on root causes and automated safeguards?"*
* *"What are the primary Service Level Objectives (SLOs) or metrics that keep your team accountable?"*

---

## 👥 4. Team Dynamics, Collaboration & Mentorship

These questions evaluate whether you will enjoy working with the team day-to-day:

* *"How are major architectural decisions made? Do you write Design Docs / RFCs and review them collaboratively, or is it decided by senior leadership?"*
* *"How are code reviews conducted? Are there automated linters/formatters so reviewers can focus on logic, scalability, and security rather than style?"*
* *"What does success look like for an engineer in this role during their first 90 days?"*

---

## 👔 5. For Engineering Managers or Directors

If your interviewer is an EM or Director:

* *"How do you support your engineers' career progression and identify high-impact projects for them?"*
* *"What is the biggest challenge your organization is facing over the next 12 to 18 months?"*
* *"How do cross-functional teams (Product, Design, Data, Infrastructure) collaborate with Engineering when roadmaps change?"*

---

## ⚠️ Questions to AVOID Asking

❌ *"Did I pass?"* or *"How did I do?"* (Puts the interviewer in an uncomfortable spot; hiring decisions are made in debrief committees).  
❌ *"What does your company do?"* (Reveals zero preparation).  
❌ Questions easily answered on the company's public careers page or blog.  
❌ Compensation, vacation days, or perks (Save these for the recruiter/HR call).
