# 🎯 Grind 75 Study Roadmap & Practice Tracker

> Inspired by **Yangshun Tay's [Tech Interview Handbook](https://github.com/yangshun/tech-interview-handbook)** and **[Grind 75](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/grind75/)**.

Grind 75 is the modernized, ROI-ranked evolution of the legendary **Blind 75**. It prioritizes questions by **concept dependency**, **frequency in tier-1 product interviews (Google, Meta, Amazon, Apple, Netflix)**, and **depth of algorithmic learning**.

---

## ⏱️ Recommended Study Timelines

* ⚡ **4-Week Intensive Sprint (20 hrs/week)**: Complete Week 1 to Week 4 (Core patterns: Two Pointers, Sliding Window, Trees, Graphs).
* 🎯 **8-Week Standard Track (10 hrs/week)**: Solve ~9–10 questions per week following the sequential order below.
* 🛡️ **16-Week Comprehensive Mastery (5 hrs/week)**: 1 problem per day, combining live coding + dry-run trace table verification.
